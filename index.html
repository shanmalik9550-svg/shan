Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions regarding multi-brand kitchen stove, hob, and chimney repairs.

How fast does your technician arrive after booking?
Our mobile technician fleet is positioned across all major metropolitan areas and zones. We typically arrive at your doorstep within 30 to 45 minutes of booking confirmation for prompt same-day repairs.
What warranty do you provide on repairs and replacement parts?
All our kitchen appliance repair services are backed by a comprehensive 90-day doorstep service warranty covering both technician labor and genuine spare parts replaced.
Which appliance brands do you service?
We specialize in out-of-warranty doorstep servicing for Glen, Bosch, Elica, Faber, Gilma, Hafele, Kaff, Siemens, Blowhot, Prestige, Sunflame, Hindware, Whirlpool, and all major Indian and European kitchen appliance brands.
Do you use genuine and original spare parts?
Yes, our engineers exclusively fit high-grade, model-matched replacement components including forged brass burner tops, safety thermocouple valves, electronic spark generators, and thermal sensors.
What are your diagnostic and inspection charges?
Doorstep diagnosis starts at just ₹299. If you approve the technician's estimate and proceed with the repair work, this diagnostic fee is 100% adjusted into your final repair bill!
What should I do during an urgent gas leak or hazard?
Shut off your main cylinder knob or pipeline shutoff valve immediately. Keep kitchen windows wide open. Avoid flicking any light switches or electrical appliances, and call our 24/7 hotline at 1800-202-6208 right away.
